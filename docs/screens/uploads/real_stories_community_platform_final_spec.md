# Real Stories Community Platform --- Final Product & Technical Specification

## 1. Executive Summary

This product is the digital home for a large Facebook community built
around marriage, family life, relationships, personal experiences, and
practical advice.

The community already has a strong and distinctive content model:

-   A woman creator/storyteller runs a Facebook page with 200,000+
    followers/readers.
-   People contact her privately to share real marriage and relationship
    experiences.
-   They usually call her directly, often over multiple conversations,
    or send WhatsApp voice notes.
-   Conversations may happen in English or local languages and may be
    spread across multiple calls/voice notes.
-   People rarely type their entire stories because they are often
    emotional and find it easier to speak.
-   The creator listens, asks questions, understands the context, and
    later writes the story herself in English.
-   Stories are published anonymously, often in multiple parts.
-   She also publishes her own experiences and provides advice,
    reflections, questions, and community content.
-   Facebook is currently the primary distribution channel.
-   The proposed app should primarily serve readers/community members:
    reading, discovering, participating, receiving notifications, and
    supporting the creator.

The product should **not attempt to replace the human story-submission
process**.

The calls and WhatsApp voice notes are part of what makes the community
authentic.

### Core positioning

> **She is the storyteller/editor. The community is the audience. The
> app is the community's home.**

The long-term product should become an owned digital ecosystem around
the creator and her community:

``` text
Facebook
    ↓
Discovery / Distribution
    ↓
Public Website
    ↓
Story Archive / SEO / Conversion
    ↓
Mobile App
    ↓
Community / Reading / Notifications / Support
    ↓
Creator Ecosystem
    ├── Events
    ├── Courses
    ├── Workbooks
    ├── Expert sessions
    ├── Support / Donations
    └── Future marketplace/services
```

# 2. Product Vision

Build a trusted digital community where people can:

-   Read real relationship and family stories.
-   Discover stories by topic.
-   Follow ongoing story series.
-   Discuss experiences with other community members.
-   Ask questions.
-   Participate in polls.
-   Receive notifications when new stories or parts are published.
-   Save stories for later.
-   Support the creator/community financially.
-   Easily contact the creator to share their own experiences through
    calls or WhatsApp.

The platform should eventually evolve from a content archive into a
broader relationship and family-life ecosystem.

### Long-term vision

> **A trusted African community platform built around real stories,
> relationships, family life, conversation, and practical wisdom.**

# 3. Product Principles

## 3.1 Do not automate the part that makes her special

The most valuable part of the existing process is human.

Preserve:

-   Listening
-   Empathy
-   Human conversation
-   Follow-up questions
-   Understanding context
-   Editorial judgment
-   Storytelling
-   Her personal voice
-   Her relationship with the audience

Automate:

-   Publishing
-   Organization
-   Search
-   Categorization
-   Recommendations
-   Notifications
-   Analytics
-   Payments
-   Community management
-   Transcription
-   Draft assistance
-   Content formatting
-   Distribution

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.2 Information is free. Transformation is paid.

Core stories should remain free initially.

The product should monetize deeper experiences rather than people's
pain.

Potential future paid experiences:

-   Workshops
-   Events
-   Courses
-   Relationship challenges
-   Workbooks
-   Expert sessions
-   Private groups
-   Premium Q&A
-   Carefully selected services
-   Sponsorships
-   Affiliate commerce

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3.3 Authenticity is a competitive advantage

The community should communicate a clear identity:

> **Real people. Real experiences. Real stories.**

The fact that contributors speak directly with the creator is an
important part of the brand.

She is not simply a content administrator.

She is effectively:

-   Storyteller
-   Interviewer
-   Listener
-   Editor
-   Community leader
-   Advisor
-   Trusted confidant

The product should reinforce this identity.

# 4. Existing Story Submission Process

The existing workflow should remain the primary source of stories.

``` text
Person has an experience
        ↓
Calls the creator
OR
Sends WhatsApp voice notes
        ↓
They talk through the situation
        ↓
Creator asks questions
        ↓
Multiple calls / voice notes
        ↓
Creator understands the context
        ↓
Creator processes the experience
        ↓
Creator writes the story in English
        ↓
Story published
        ↓
Community reads
        ↓
Community discusses
        ↓
Community supports creator
```

A single story may involve:

-   One phone call
-   Several phone calls
-   One WhatsApp voice note
-   Many WhatsApp voice notes
-   A combination of calls and voice notes
-   Follow-up conversations

The app should therefore **not require contributors to write their
stories**.

# 15. Anonymous Identity Model

Anonymous stories should protect contributors.

The system should distinguish between:

### Private identity

Stored securely:

-   Real name
-   Phone number
-   Contact information
-   Internal contributor ID
-   Consent information
-   Conversation/source metadata

### Public identity

Displayed publicly:

-   Anonymous Wife
-   Anonymous Husband
-   Anonymous Sister
-   Anonymous Friend
-   Anonymous Mother
-   Anonymous Man
-   Anonymous Woman
-   Custom pseudonym

Example:

``` text
Private:

Contributor #10291
Name: [private]
Phone: [private]

Public:

Anonymous Wife
```

The public identity must never expose the private contributor record.

# 89. Final Recommendation

Build the product around what already works rather than trying to change
the community's behavior.

The creator's biggest asset is not the Facebook page alone.

It is the **trust relationship she has built with her audience and the
people who personally come to her with their stories**.

Therefore:

1.  Keep calls and WhatsApp voice notes as the primary story-submission
    channel.
2.  Do not force contributors to type stories.
3.  Make the mobile app primarily a reading and community experience.
4.  Make multi-part stories a core feature.
5.  Build a powerful searchable story archive.
6.  Keep the core stories free.
7.  Use voluntary support/donations as the first monetization mechanism.
8.  Add paid experiences only after strong community engagement exists.
9.  Keep the creator's editorial voice and judgment at the center.
10. Use AI first to help the creator process, organize and write from
    conversations---not to replace the human relationship.
11. Keep private contributor identity and source material strictly
    separated from public content.
12. Use Facebook for discovery and distribution, but build the website
    and app as the owned home.
13. Build the technical foundation so that future AI, courses, events,
    expert services and marketplace capabilities can be added without
    redesigning the core platform.

The fundamental product idea is:

> **Don't build an app for people to submit stories. Build a digital
> home for the community that already exists.**

And the long-term product philosophy is:

> **Preserve the human conversation. Automate everything around it.**
